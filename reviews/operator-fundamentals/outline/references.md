# Planned references

These are the sources phase 2 should open before a claim is quoted. A URL here is a planned starting point, not a statement that the page was re-read for this outline.

## Added module — First principles

- **OpenStax Chemistry 2e.** Atoms and electrons. Open the relevant section before quoting it in a lesson. https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e
- **OpenStax College Physics 2e.** Charge, voltage, current, resistance, energy, magnetism, induction, temperature, and heat. Book home. Lesson drafts will cite the section actually opened. https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e

## Spec module 1 — Introduction to power generation

- **U.S. Energy Information Administration, Electricity explained.** How electricity is generated and how it is delivered. Confirm the page text when the lesson is written. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/

## Spec module 11 — Industrial safety fundamentals

- **OSHA, personal protective equipment.** PPE as a site-specified control. Do not copy a rule number until the page is opened. https://www.osha.gov/personal-protective-equipment
- **OSHA, control of hazardous energy.** Lockout as a concept. The course does not teach a person to apply a lock. https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy
- **OSHA, electrical.** Electrical hazard recognition, including arc flash as a named hazard. https://www.osha.gov/electrical
- **OSHA, confined spaces.** Recognition that a confined space is a qualified task. https://www.osha.gov/confined-spaces
- **OSHA, fall protection.** Work at heights as a recognized hazard. https://www.osha.gov/fall-protection

## Spec module 4 — Electrical fundamentals for operators

- **OpenStax College Physics 2e.** Alternating current, induction, and the pole-speed relationship as physics. https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e
- **EIA, delivery to consumers.** Why voltage is changed between generation and customers. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/delivery-to-consumers.php

## Spec module 3 — How a gas turbine works

- **EIA, how electricity is generated.** Simple-cycle and combined-cycle turbines as classes of plant. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/how-electricity-is-generated.php
- **U.S. Department of Energy.** A public gas-turbine explainer, to be chosen and opened in phase 2. No limit will be taken from an unopened page. https://www.energy.gov/

## Spec module 2 — Understanding the facility

- **The plant's OEM equipment manual.** Authority for that machine. This outline does not cite a manual number, a drawing number, or a setpoint. Public explainers are listed on the gas-turbine module.

## Spec module 5 — Plant operating conditions

- **NERC.** Glossary terms for availability, if a definition is quoted, only after the glossary entry is opened. Plant procedures still govern the local word. https://www.nerc.com/

## Spec module 8 — Instrumentation and control

- **OpenStax College Physics 2e.** The measured quantities. A controls textbook is out of scope. https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e

## Spec module 6 — Basic startup and shutdown concepts

- **The plant's approved start and shutdown procedure.** Governs real action. This course teaches the purpose of each stage and does not copy a procedure.

## Spec module 10 — Power industry and energy markets

- **FERC, electric power markets.** What a wholesale market is. No trading strategy. https://www.ferc.gov/electric
- **ERCOT, about.** Labeled example of one region. Not a universal rulebook. https://www.ercot.com/about
- **PJM.** Labeled example of a second region. Confirm any quoted sentence against the page. https://www.pjm.com/
- **EIA, efficiency FAQ.** Public heat-rate idea. Not a plant heat rate. https://www.eia.gov/tools/faqs/faq.php?id=107&t=3
- **NERC.** Reliability as a standards idea. Do not cite a standard number until the standard is opened. https://www.nerc.com/

## Spec module 7 — Operator responsibilities

- **OSHA, emergency action and hazard communication topic pages.** Duties that touch emergencies stay at recognition and reporting. https://www.osha.gov/

## Spec module 9 — Common problems and abnormal conditions

- **The plant's alarm and trip documentation.** Names the real limits. This course does not copy an alarm list or a model-specific cause.

## Spec module 12 — Practical operator knowledge and terminology

- **The sources of the earlier modules.** This module reinforces terms already tied to a source. It does not add a new technical claim. https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/
